- docs/CHANGELOG.md

Behaviour added:
- Combat_Evidence_Debug.txt, Combat_Correlation_Debug.txt and Combat_Episode_Debug.txt synchronize from processed snapshot cache after History catch-up.
- Manual Update History can perform this synchronization even when no new saves are waiting.
- Live History keeps queue-first scheduling and synchronizes combat diagnostics only after the queue reaches zero.
- Cache-only synchronization refuses raw .sav fallback.
- War Journal / Timeline / Scribes cached evidence ignores archived-but-unprocessed snapshots.

No database schema change, parsed-cache component version bump, archived-save rewrite or full cache rebuild is required.
