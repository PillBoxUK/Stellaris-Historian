Stellaris Historian v0.0.82 — The Civic & Frontier Chronicle

INSTALLATION — CLASSIC FLAT-ROOT PATCH
- Close Historian after Live History has finished and is OFF.
- Back up data/ and your installation before applying.
- Extract directly into G:\codex\StellarisHistorian, overwrite application files.
- Start the original root start.bat; confirm v0.0.82.
- With Live History OFF, Manage Campaign → Update Historical Timeline
  to backfill political/infrastructure evidence from archived saves.
- New saves are indexed incrementally; no canonical database migration.
- A full backfill revisits the archive and may take considerable time.

LIMITATIONS

- No GitHub changes.
